DueDrop Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

1. Overview

DueDrop is a privacy-first bill reminder app. Your bills, amounts, notes, categories and attachments are stored only on your device. We do not run accounts and we do not upload your financial data to our servers.

2. Data you enter

Bill details, reminders, categories and attachments you create are stored locally in the app's database on your device. Attachments are copied into the app's private storage. This data leaves your device only when you explicitly export or back it up, and it goes only where you choose to send it.

3. Purchases

DueDrop Premium is a one-time purchase. Payments are processed by our reseller and Merchant of Record. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded and the email you provide, which we use to issue and email your license key. We do not receive or store your card, bank, or e-wallet credentials.

4. Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and use them to show ads, including personalized ads where permitted. In regions that require it (EEA, UK, Switzerland) and for US state privacy laws, you are asked for consent through Google's consent tool before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice in Settings. Premium removes ads. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Notification content (bill name, amount, due date) can be limited in Settings for privacy on your lock screen.

6. Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect your bills, amounts, categories, notes, or attachments. We do not track your location, contacts, or usage of other apps.

7. Children

DueDrop is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above and shipping an app update.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy or the app? Contact us at supportduedrop@gmail.com.

This policy describes how DueDrop handles your information.
